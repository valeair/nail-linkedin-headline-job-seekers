## 1. ⚠️ Why “Open to Work” Is Not Enough

The green #OpenToWork frame signals availability, but it does **nothing for discoverability**. Recruiters search for skills and job titles, not availability status.

| If you write…    | Recruiters see…    | Result                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------- |
| “Open to work”   | Nothing searchable | You never appear in results |
| “Audit Associate | CPA eligible       | SOX compliance”             |

**Fix:** Replace the generic banner with a keyword‑rich headline. Use the frame as a bonus, not a crutch.

---

## 2. 📱 The 40‑Character Mobile Preview (Your Hook)

On mobile, only the first **40 characters** show before truncation. That’s your only chance to stop the scroll.

**Weak hook (first 40 chars):**  
`Experienced professional seeking new…` (generic, wasted)

**Strong hook (first 40 chars):**  
`I deliver projects on time & under budget 🎯 |…` (outcome, stands out)

**Rule:** Lead with a result or a bold statement. Save keywords for the remaining 180 characters.

## 4. 🚫 Three Mistakes That Kill Job Seeker Headlines

### Mistake 1 – Writing “Student at University”

- **Why it fails:** Recruiters search for roles, not demographics.
- **Fix:** `Aspiring [Job Title] | [Skills] | [Proof]`

### Mistake 2 – Using internal jargon (“Dept. 42 Lead”)

- **Why it fails:** No one outside your company understands it.
- **Fix:** Translate to industry standard: `Supply Chain Manager`

### Mistake 3 – Buzzwords instead of evidence

- **Why it fails:** “Passionate ninja” won’t pass ATS filters.
- **Fix:** `PMP Certified | Managed $2M budget`

---

## 5. 🔑 The Hook + Keyword Blueprint

A high‑performing headline has two halves:

| Half                           | Purpose                       | Example                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **First 40 characters (Hook)** | Stop the scroll, show outcome | `I deliver projects on time 🎯`                         |
| **Remaining (Keywords)**       | Get found in searches         | `PMP Certified PM | Cloud migrations | Open to Austin` |

**Your turn:** Write a hook about what you actually deliver. Then add 2‑3 hard skills and a location/availability.

---

## 6. ✅ Quick Checklist Before You Publish

- [ ] First 40 characters contain a hook (outcome or bold claim)
- [ ] At least two hard skills or certifications mentioned
- [ ] No “open to work” as the only text
- [ ] No internal jargon or buzzwords (“synergy”, “ninja”)
- [ ] Under 220 characters (paste into LinkedIn to verify)

If all boxes are checked, you’ve already beaten 80% of job seekers.
